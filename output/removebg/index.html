Frequently Asked Questions

Is this background remover really free?

Yes. RemoveBG is 100% free with no registration, no watermark and no usage limits. The AI segmentation model runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

Never. All processing happens locally on your device. Your images are never transmitted, stored or inspected by any remote server.

What formats and sizes are supported?

PNG, JPG, JPEG and WebP up to 15MB are supported. The result is exported as a high-quality transparent PNG, ready for e-commerce, thumbnails or design work.
