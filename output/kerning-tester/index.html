Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kerning in typography?

Kerning refers to the spacing between two specific characters, adjusted to create an even, harmonious visual appearance.

Why is optical kerning important for branding?

Mathematical spacing often leaves awkward gaps between letters with slants or serifs (like W, A, V, T). Proper optical kerning ensures professional logos and titles.
