Frequently Asked Questions

      

        
Is this PDF to Markdown converter free?

        
Yes, it is 100% free with no registration, no watermark and no usage limits. All processing happens locally in your browser using Mozilla's pdf.js.

      

      

        
Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

        
Never. Your PDF is parsed on-device and never transmitted, stored or inspected by any remote server.

      

      

        
Does it work with scanned PDFs?

        
It extracts the embedded text layer of digital PDFs. Scanned or image-only PDFs without a text layer are not supported by this converter.
